# Data Visualiser

Live: https://laurencefwhite.github.io/data-visualiser/

This repository holds the published site only.

## Data and credits

- Country boundaries: Natural Earth, admin-0 countries at 1:50 million (Gibraltar and the US Minor Outlying
  Islands at 1:10 million), public domain. Boundaries are de facto, as Natural Earth draws them.
- The 2019 sample is Natural Earth's own attributes (2019 population and GDP estimates, income groups).
- Population by country and year: Our World in Data, from HYDE (2023), Gapminder (2022) and the UN World
  Population Prospects (2024), with the UN medium projections from 2024 to 2100. CC BY 4.0.
- Samples over time, all via Our World in Data, CC BY 4.0: CO₂, Global Carbon Budget (2025); life expectancy,
  Riley (2005), Zijdeman et al. (2015), Human Mortality Database (2025) and UN WPP (2024); children per woman,
  Human Fertility Database (2026) and UN WPP (2024); GDP per person, access to electricity and income groups,
  World Bank (2026).
- Colour schemes after ColorBrewer (Cynthia Brewer, Apache 2.0), viridis and magma (CC0) and Tableau 10.
- SheetJS Community Edition (Apache 2.0), d3 (ISC) and topojson-client (BSD).
